# Stock behaviour: what OS 1.54 does with notes, pitch bend, aftertouch and the
# mod wheel arriving at MIDI IN. MIDI track A selected, CHAN on = 1.
# Auto channel 14 (status nibble D), track A's channel 9 (nibble 8).
mark auto: note
midi 9d 3c 64
wait 200
midi 8d 3c 00
wait 100
mark auto: pitch bend up, max, centre
midi ed 00 60
midi ed 7f 7f
midi ed 00 40
mark auto: channel aftertouch
midi dd 50
mark auto: mod wheel CC1
midi bd 01 40
wait 100
mark track A channel 9: note
midi 98 3e 64
wait 200
midi 88 3e 00
wait 100
mark track A channel 9: pitch bend
midi e8 00 60
mark track A channel 9: aftertouch
midi d8 50
mark track A channel 9: mod wheel CC1
midi b8 01 40
wait 300
snap after
